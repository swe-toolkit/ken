## Objective

Derive and validate the missing planner-owned checked-IH CONTINUATION-INHERITANCE
relation: a SUCCESSOR PROJECTION of one existing `CheckedIhEnvironmentTransport`
that proves, by a forward planner walk carrying the active computational-frame
lineage, TWO separate facts:

- **Inheritance.** The exact captured continuation environment / call capability
  `K` (authorized by one existing transport) remains in scope at the exact
  recursively-exposed zero-argument checked self-resumption invocation.
- **Fresh-result destination.** Conditional on applying `K` at that invocation,
  the FRESH result `R2` is destined for the eventual ordinary `ITree::Ret`
  constructor-child binder and its exact lexical-closure capture occurrence.

The two proofs are SEPARATE — never one transitive pairing, and never a claim
that the earlier D3A result `R1` reaches the later capture. The relation closes
inductively over repeated self-resumption. The node produces the relation (both
halves, exposed separately), one exact keyed accessor, and its validator
controls. It emits nothing and changes no runtime behavior; its correctness is
the inheritance-plus-destination split, not any product.

   `CheckedIhEnvironmentTransport`. Its subject is checked-IH CONTINUATION
   INHERITANCE, never dynamic result succession. It mints NO environment
   transport, call identity, binder catalog, aggregate record, numeric identity,
   ABI lane, or result. Its KEY is the existing exact transport/call identity PLUS
   the full descendant checked invocation coordinate. `worker_body_origin=None` is
   only OPTIONAL refinement behavior of the accessor, never candidate/first-match
   authority. Ambiguity fails closed; numeric origins remain report coordinates.
2. Derive it as TWO SEPARATE proofs, never one transitive pairing:
   - **(a) INHERITANCE** — the exact `K` remains in scope at the exact
     recursively-exposed zero-argument checked self-resumption invocation, derived
     by a FORWARD active-frame / self-resumption walk from the transport's
     destination construct, IN ORDER: exact transport -> destination construct
     field -> `TerminalResumeOuter` active frame -> declared recursive child ->
     exact checked self-resumption invocation. Do NOT add a reverse
     parent/body/environment search.
   - **(b) FRESH-RESULT DESTINATION** — conditional on applying `K` at that
     invocation, the FRESH result `R2` is destined for the eventual ordinary Ret
     case -> Ret constructor-child binder -> exact lexical-closure capture
     occurrence and its existing `BoundaryClosureEnvironment` record. This proof
     contains NO `R1` identity and asserts NO value equality between `R1` and
     `R2`.
   These may be separate fields/views of one projection, but the validator and
   accessor MUST expose the distinction. Any record explainable ONLY as "`R1`
   reaches this capture" is rejected.
3. Close INDUCTIVELY over repeated self-resumption: the SAME typed rule applies at
   every depth with no additional metadata layer. The read/write two-instance
   witnesses are WITNESSES, not the closure proof; do NOT freeze the read/write
   origin chains as the recursion law. Prove structural closure from the forward
   walk and include depth-varied controls.
4. Extend the EXISTING binder-provenance walk rather than adding a second binder
   catalog: retain `CheckedCaseBinderLayout` as the SOLE binder-order authority,
   but PRESERVE the exact ordinary `ConstructorChild` provenance needed at the Ret

   `Ordinary`. `CheckedIhBinding(None)` remains a required NEGATIVE control (the
   Ret occurrence is an ordinary constructor-child binder, not an IH).
5. Preserve zero-argument versus one-parameter. The reached checked-IH invocation
   applies `K` with ZERO source arguments; the later ordinary boundary closure
   declares ONE parameter and remains a distinct one-parameter source operation.
   Do NOT call that closure at the zero-argument site, invent its parameter, or
   reinterpret the zero-argument invocation as the later closure call.
6. The predeclared closure environment record stays OWNED by its canonical

- AC-FRESH-DEST-DERIVE (fresh-R2 destination, read/write separate) — conditional
  on applying `K` at that invocation, derive that the FRESH result `R2` is

  accessor expose the `K`-inheritance proof and the fresh-`R2`-destination proof
  as SEPARATE fields/views. A record explainable ONLY as "`R1` reaches this
  capture" is REJECTED by the validator.
- AC-SUCC-INDUCTIVE (inductive / depth closure) — the relation closes inductively

  no additional metadata layer. Prove structural closure from the forward walk and
  include DEPTH-VARIED controls. The read/write instances are witnesses, NOT the

  law.
- AC-SUCC-REDERIVE (validator equality) — re-derive the closed relation (BOTH

- AC-SUCC-KEY (exact keyed accessor) — the one accessor is keyed by the existing

- AC-SUCC-DISJOINT (R1 disjointness control) — substituting `R1`, OR the original

- AC-SUCC-ATMOSTONCE (at-most-once, inheritance and fresh-result binding

- AC-SUCC-EXCLUSION (positive exclusion controls) — keep an ordinary NON-governed
