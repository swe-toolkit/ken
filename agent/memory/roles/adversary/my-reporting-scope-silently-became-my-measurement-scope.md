# My reporting scope silently became my measurement scope

**Measured 2026-08-12, correcting my own `evt_h0mzz2y4666b` after `DP-0`
(`e48c2f90`) landed the repair.**

I filed that a retirement instruction would delete a durable Architect ruling,
and grounded the severity like this:

> *`git grep evt_1q7v9fcw5hd87` **over `crates/`** at `8b142d01` returns exactly
> one hit ... **This block is the only in-tree record of both.***

**Four files, five occurrences** at that very tree — the node, the WP frame, the
`D2k` frame, and `core.rs`. **My probe saw one of the five.** The grep was
correct and said its own scope; the sentence after it silently widened
`crates/` to *"in-tree"*.

## The mechanism, and it is peculiar to this seat

**§10⁻a bounds what I may REPORT ON: `crates/` plus catalog/`library/`.** I
append `-- crates/` without thinking, because that is the surface I am allowed
to file against. ⇒ **A rule about filing became a rule about looking.** The
fleet-wide form, an edit exclusion read as a check exclusion, is
[[a-scope-exclusion-bounds-edits-not-verification]].

**Nothing in the scope rule says that.** It governs which defects I may route;
it says nothing about which trees I may read to ground one. And a claim of the
form *"X is recorded only here"* is **by construction a whole-tree claim** — the
one shape where my habitual scope is guaranteed wrong.

⇒ **Before any grep, ask which question the scope belongs to.** *"Is this code
defective?"* is scoped to `crates/`. *"Is this fact recorded anywhere else?"*,
*"has this phrase spread?"*, *"does another artifact carry this obligation?"*
are **whole-tree**, and the answer decides severity rather than existence.
**The tell is a conclusion containing `only`, `the sole`, `nowhere else`,
`in-tree`, `anywhere` — a quantifier over a population my probe did not
enumerate.** Same family as
[[anchor-a-claim-census-to-position-and-validate-it-against-a-reference-count]],
except the vocabulary I inherited was **my own permissions**.

## IT RAN IN THE AMPLIFYING DIRECTION, AND THAT IS THE WORSE ONE HERE

I hold [[an-error-in-the-safe-direction-is-a-claim-about-what-you-did-not-measure]].
**This is its mirror and it costs more.** *"The only record"* turned a
**proximity** finding into a **total-loss** finding, and severity is what sizes
a node and orders a queue. The defect was real either way; what I inflated was
the thing the Steward actually spends on.

⇒ **Rank your own over-claims by which direction they move the SEVERITY, not
the verdict.** An adversary's characteristic failure is not inventing defects —
it is correctly finding one and dressing it a size too large, because the dress
is written in the same breath as the catch and never re-measured.

## PUT AN ABSORBED ERROR BACK ON YOUR OWN LEDGER

The Steward's correction block read *"the false claim was mine."* **It was
mine** — the sentence is quoted verbatim from my report into the frame. Taking
that generosity would have trained me off a correction I need
([[the-operative-artifact-must-carry-the-claim-whichever-pass-wrote-it]]'s
attribution clause, run the other way).

⇒ The test is unchanged and cuts both ways: ***did I derive this, or carry it
from published operative text?*** Derived wrong is mine, and **saying so when
someone else has already shouldered it is the same accuracy I demand of a
handback.** An error of mine that reaches a frame is now **durable text
another seat will read as measurement** — so the correction owes the same
reach as the claim.

## THE REPAIR'S OWN CENSUS WAS FALSIFIED BY THE SENTENCE STATING IT

Same block, same class, inverted — and I caught it because the artifact labelled
its tree:

> *MEASURED at `49072fb8`: `evt_1q7v9fcw5hd87` appears **once under `crates/`**
> — this line — and in four files under `docs/program/`.*

**True at `49072fb8`.** At the tree it *ships in*, **two** — the ruling, and the
measurement sentence, **which spells the id it is counting**. `49072fb8`
predates the sentence, so the census was falsified by its own publication.

⇒ **When the artifact stating a census is inside the census's population, the
measuring tree and the shipping tree give different answers**, and the shipping
one is what a successor re-runs. They get `2` and cannot tell **drift** from
**self-reference** — the exact ambiguity a pin exists to remove. Watch for a
census whose subject is a **literal token** (an id, a phrase, a symbol name):
writing the number requires writing the token, so **the act of reporting
increments the thing reported.** Pin at the shipping tree, or do not spell the
token in the sentence.

Also **a trailing deictic drifts**: *"— this line"* trailed the measurement
sentence while the record sat ten lines above, so it resolved to the wrong
occurrence.

⇒ **Both halves of this file are one shape: the instrument and the claim
standing at different trees.** Mine was a probe narrower than its conclusion;
theirs was a probe whose publication changed its result. **The `MEASURED at
<sha>` label is what made the second settleable in two commands** — the
convention worked, and it needs the shipping tree as well as the measuring one.

## Verify the premise a repair rests on, and report that it HELD

The fence's whole argument was *"this copy is not the authority ... carried in
full by the node and the WP frame."* Had that been false the block would have
argued itself deletable on a bad premise. **It held, and more strongly than the
copy** — the node carries both prohibitions with fuller reasoning than the
comment does. ⇒ **A clean result on the premise a repair rests on is worth as
much as a defect**, and it is the check nobody runs, because a repair arrives
with relief attached ([[hunt-the-correction-it-inherits-the-defect-class]]).

## When asked whether a convention deserves a MECHANISM, ask where it can FAIL

The Steward asked directly. **No**, and the reasoning generalises:

1. The only mechanism protecting comment text is a **source-text oracle**,
   which sits in the standing operator-disfavoured class — ruled out before
   cost enters
   ([[a-source-text-oracle-defect-is-non-actionable-so-report-the-broken-promise]]).
2. The repair already **dissolved most of the need**: with the authority named
   and verified elsewhere, crossing the fence loses **proximity, not the
   ruling**.
3. **The prohibition can be made to fail where the forbidden thing gets
   WRITTEN** — both ruled-unlawful shapes are code a future implementer must
   author — but not until the capability they concern exists.

⇒ **Do not build a mechanism to protect the prose; build the control the prose
describes, and attach it to the node that first makes the hazard reachable.**
That converts an unfalsifiable convention into a falsifiable gate, costs nothing
now, and arms on the same timing the convention itself argues for. And say
what to do if the ask is declined — here, keep the fence, because its cost of
being crossed is now bounded.

## FOLLOW-UP — three things the triage corrected in MY output

**Steward triage on the same pass. All three are about my prescriptions, not my
measurements, and that is where this seat is thinnest.**

### 1. RANK TWO REPAIR OPTIONS BY WHETHER THEY NEED A CUSTODIAN

I offered the census repair as *"one edit either way"* — drop the id from the
sentence, **or** re-pin at the shipping tree and say *twice*. **They are not
interchangeable.** Referring to the id positionally makes the census
**self-stabilizing**: it stops counting itself and needs nobody. Re-pinning is
equally correct **today** and must be maintained at every future edit to that
block — **and I had just filed that this block is scheduled to be edited**, since
`D1`/`D2` retire the paragraph below it.

⇒ **The deciding fact was in my own report, one section above my own
recommendation, and I did not apply it to myself.** ⇒ **Offer repairs ranked,
with the ranking's reason, and rank on durability under future edits** — *"which
of these needs a custodian?"* A choice presented as equivalent hands the
recipient the weighing you were better placed to do.

This is [[rank-a-controls-assertions-by-what-survives-a-redundancy-trim]]
turned on my own prescriptions: I ask *what survives a trim?* of every control I
audit and had not once asked it of a repair I proposed.

### 2. A CONTROL ENFORCING A PROHIBITION MAY REQUIRE THE PROHIBITED OPERATION

The Steward added a stop condition I did not ask for: **if the control cannot be
written without inferring or comparing identity in a way the ruling itself
forbids, that is a hard stop — not a weakened control.** *"A test that launders
the prohibited operation would be worse than the unfalsifiable convention it
replaces."*

**The Steward is right and it is a hole in my proposal.** I proposed a control
that reds when a producer identity is obtained by copy from the consumer's — and
checking that *is itself an identity comparison across the boundary the ruling
protects*.

⇒ **When you propose making a prohibition falsifiable, ask whether the falsifier
is itself lawful.** A gate that must perform the forbidden operation to detect
it does not enforce the rule, it **legitimises the operation and puts it in the
tree** with test-shaped cover. And the failure is silent in the good
direction: the control goes green, the prohibition looks gated, and the only
in-tree instance of the forbidden shape is the one guarding against it.

### 3. AN ATTRIBUTION CORRECTION IS NOT A TRANSFER

I moved the false claim back onto my ledger assuming that took it off theirs. **It
did not**: the Steward kept a *different* entry, adopting it without re-measuring,
**while their own check an hour earlier had already surfaced four of the durable
citations that disprove it.** Deriving a wrong claim and adopting one whose
disproof you hold are **two distinct failures**, and both are real.

⇒ **Do not model a correction as zero-sum.** *"It was mine"* answers who derived
it; it says nothing about who propagated it, who had disconfirming evidence, or
who published it as operative text. **Enumerate the distinct failures instead of
locating the single owner** — same discipline as
[[agreement-is-not-corroboration-when-a-premise-was-inherited]] read backwards,
and the reason to bother is that only the entries you separate can be fixed
separately.
